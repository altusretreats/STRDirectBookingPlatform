# Altus Retreats Guest Guide Knowledge Base

> **Purpose**
>
> This document is the durable editorial knowledge base for guest recommendations across Altus Retreats properties. It keeps standardized descriptions, AI context, and writing guidance available to future listings, guest guides, websites, and marketing work without relying on chat history.

## How this document is used

- Treat this file as the editorial working source for recommendation copy and context.
- Keep every recommendation scoped to a property. Shared guidance may be reused, but live guidebook records must still use the correct `propertyId`.
- Publish guest-facing content through the Admin Guidebook. Editing this file does not update the live site, the public guidebook API, the agent-context feed, or the Hospitable knowledge PDF.
- Keep private access details, contact information, door codes, Wi-Fi credentials, reservation data, and host-only notes out of this document.
- Update this file when a recommendation is added, materially revised, or retired.

## AI writing guidelines

When creating or updating guest recommendations:

- Keep guest-facing descriptions between **140–165 characters** unless otherwise specified.
- Write in a warm, premium, concierge-style tone.
- Avoid sounding like advertising.
- Focus on what makes the recommendation memorable.
- Mention signature items or experiences guests consistently rave about.
- When applicable, mention whether it is a local favorite, iconic, or a must-visit.
- Avoid repeating the same adjectives across entries.
- Make descriptions work equally well for:
  - Airbnb guidebooks
  - Vrbo guidebooks
  - Direct booking websites
  - Mobile guest guides

Every recommendation should answer:

1. Why should a guest go?
2. What is it famous for?
3. Who is it best for?
4. What makes it different from everything else nearby?

#### Sky Bridge Arch

**Category:** Hiking / Natural arch

**Guest description**

> Walk above and beneath Red River Gorge's most popular sandstone arch on a short loop with broad overlooks, a cliffside shelf, and dramatic forest views.

**Guest content**

Sky Bridge Trail #214 is a moderate, approximately one-mile loop from the Sky Bridge parking area. The trail follows a high, narrow ridge to scenic overlooks, crosses over the natural sandstone arch, passes beneath it along a high cliff shelf, and climbs 75 stairs back toward the parking area. The top of the bridge is wide but has no guardrail, so keep children close, leash pets, and stay well back from edges. The recreation area has parking, picnic tables, and accessible vault toilets, although the trail itself is not accessible. Check current Forest Service alerts before leaving.

#### Nada Tunnel

**Category:** Scenic drive / Historic landmark

**Guest description**

> Drive through a 900-foot, one-lane tunnel blasted through solid rock for a logging railroad—and call it “NAY-duh” if you want to sound like a local.

**Guest content**

Nada Tunnel is an iconic gateway into Red River Gorge on KY 77. Built in 1910–1911 for a narrow-gauge logging railroad, the passage runs roughly 900 feet through solid rock and later became a one-lane roadway. The tunnel is only about 12 feet wide with limited vertical clearance. Turn on your headlights, make sure the tunnel is clear before entering, and be prepared to yield to oncoming traffic. One final local tip: it is pronounced **“NAY-duh,”** not “NAH-duh.”

#### Kentucky Reptile Zoo

**Category:** Family attraction / Education

**Guest description**

> Meet venomous snakes, giant constrictors, turtles, and alligators, then catch a live venom extraction showing how reptile science helps save lives.

**Guest content**

The Kentucky Reptile Zoo in Slade houses one of the world's largest collections of venomous snakes alongside constrictors, lizards, turtles, tortoises, and alligators. Its venom program supplies material for pharmaceutical research and antiserum production, making the visit equal parts wildlife encounter and science lesson. Scheduled programs may include a live venom extraction or an educational reptile talk with a nonvenomous animal. Hours are seasonal and extraction schedules can change at short notice, so check the official site and call the morning of your visit if seeing a demonstration is important.

#### Creation Falls & Rock Bridge

> See Creation Falls and Rock Bridge on one shaded creek loop—a wide sandstone cascade beside the Gorge's only natural arch spanning flowing water.

**Guest content**

Creation Falls and Rock Bridge sit next to each other along Rock Bridge Trail #207, a moderate loop of roughly 1.3–1.5 miles through shaded forest. The low, wide waterfall spills over a sandstone ledge into a scenic pool and is especially photogenic after recent rain, although flow can be lighter during dry weather.
